Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use the Service, we collect the expense data you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide. We do not collect payment information or government-issued identification.

We may also collect basic technical information such as your browser type and IP address for security and diagnostic purposes.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate and improve the Service. This includes displaying your expense history, generating summaries, and maintaining your account. We do not use your financial data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the features you have signed up for.

We may use anonymised, aggregated statistics (e.g., average number of expenses per user) to understand how the Service is used — these contain no personally identifiable information.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You may delete your account at any time, after which your personal data will be removed from our systems within a reasonable timeframe, except where retention is required by law.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may use trusted service providers (such as hosting infrastructure) to operate the Service, and these providers are contractually obligated to handle your data only as directed by us.

The Service may load fonts from Google Fonts. Please refer to Google's privacy policy for information on how they handle data associated with those requests.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.app. We will do our best to respond within a reasonable timeframe.
